TERRA WEEK 3:

I wanted to make a nextbot style game so I made this
I built it on godot and drew the sprites in libresprite

In the game you can:

*You can move with WASD
*You can sprint with shift
*You can jump with space
*You can shoot your gun to protect yourself from the monster

In the game there is a monster that stalks and attacks you and if it reaches you, the game ends
You also have a stamina and it gets drained when you run by pressing shift


How to install:
You can install by clicking on the link: https://github.com/omrekinci/Forest/releases/tag/SelectTag

*Opening the game

TERRA WEEK 5:

I wanted to make a nextbot style game so I made this
I built it on godot and drew the sprites in libresprite

In the game you can:

*You can move with WASD
*You can sprint with shift
*You can jump with space
*You can shoot your gun to protect yourself from the monster
*Open your inventory with tab
*Drink Energy Drinks from your inventory with E
*Change avatars
*Play with your friends
*Speak in-game
*Buy stuff from the shop
*Find money

In the game there is a monster that stalks and attacks you and if it reaches you, the game ends
You also have a stamina and it gets drained when you run by pressing shift
You have a energy bar and if that goes out you cannot run at all
You can buy stuff by choosing with arrows and pressing enter (5 dollars for redbull and 10 dollars for ammo)
